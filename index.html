01 — about

From concept to working hardware

I'm an embedded instrumentation systems integrator with more than a decade of experience in electronics and software design. I take charge of every stage of embedded development: system conception, firmware design and testing, hardware prototyping, and final PCB design and validation. My background spans bioelectronics, sensor fusion, scientific instrumentation for astronomical observatories, autonomous platforms, and professional prototyping of new embedded product concepts.

I founded Rabbit Systems Inc., my Toronto-based embedded engineering practice, and I have delivered hardware for startups in insurtech, wearables, space robotics, and motorsport. Away from the bench I'm an avid hiker and urban cyclist, and I sculpt clay — good hands matter in this trade.
